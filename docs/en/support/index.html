Frequently asked questions

Where is my ledger stored?

Your ledger and attached receipt photos are stored on your device. No app account or bank connection is required, and your ledger is not uploaded to a developer server.

How do I move to a new device?

Export a backup, including photos, from More → Backup and Restore. Keep it safe and restore the file on your new device. Restoring replaces the current ledger, so back it up first. There is no automatic synchronization or automatic backup feature.

Is Premium a subscription?

Premium is an optional one-time in-app purchase that removes banner ads. All ledger features remain free. Purchases require iOS 15 or later. Apple provides the price shown on the purchase screen.

I purchased Premium but still see ads.

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used to purchase, then open More → Remove Ads → Restore Purchases. Check your internet connection. Contact support if the issue continues. Apple handles refunds.

Why is the price unavailable?

Check your internet connection and retry on the Premium screen. Product availability from the App Store can affect requests. If it persists, contact support with your app version and the time of the issue. The app does not start a purchase without a loaded price.

Why are no ads showing?

Ads depend on connectivity, consent and ad availability. Ledger features remain usable without ads. A privacy choices button appears in the banner area when required by the advertising SDK.

Does changing currency convert my money?

USD, KRW and JPY display and input units are supported. This does not perform exchange-rate conversion or combine multiple currencies. Check your ledger amounts and backups before changing currency.

How do I delete data or change permissions?

Manage entries and attachments in the app. Deleting the app removes its device data. Delete exported backups and separately saved photos from their own storage locations. Camera and photo permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.
