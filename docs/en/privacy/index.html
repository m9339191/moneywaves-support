Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy covers the MoneyWaves iOS app and this support website, provided by Deokjoo Moon.

Information stored on your device

The app stores entry dates, amounts, types, categories, accounts and notes, budgets, recurring schedules, card settings, receipt photos and app preferences on your device. It does not create app accounts or request bank login credentials. The developer does not collect or view your ledger or photos through a server.

Photos and file backups

Camera and photo access is used when you take or select receipt photos. The iOS ledger and photo folder managed by the app is configured to be excluded from automatic OS backups. A backup you export includes your ledger and attached photos; its handling depends on the storage location or sharing app you choose. You manage and delete exported backups and separately saved photos.

Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and consent management SDK. They may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app or device identifiers, advertising and interaction information, and crash or performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. Collection and retention depend on Google policies, SDK configuration and your choices. Ledger contents and receipt photos are not attached to ad requests. The app requests non-personalized ads, which may still process technical data. On iOS 14 and later, the app requests Apple tracking authorization before initializing the advertising SDK. If you allow it, advertising partners may use the device advertising identifier for ad measurement. Declining does not prevent use of the app or non-personalized ads and does not permit access to that advertising identifier. You can change your choice in iOS Settings. Google consent forms are provided where required, and a privacy choices button is available when required by the SDK. Verified Premium ownership stops banner ad requests from the app.

Apple purchases and restoration

Premium purchases use Apple StoreKit. Apple handles payment and account information; the developer does not receive card numbers or payment passwords. The app retrieves prices and verified purchase entitlements to enable ad removal. Apple policies govern purchases, refunds, restoration and Apple retention.

Updates and review requests

The app queries Apple public lookup services or public TestFlight release information hosted on GitHub to check for updates. Queries may include the app identifier and country; providers may process connection information such as IP addresses. No ledger, receipt or separately generated user identifier is attached to these requests. First-use dates, review request dates and update prompt history are stored on your device. Apple handles review display and reviews you submit.

Support requests and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond. Please omit sensitive ledger and receipt details. Inquiry information is processed for support and necessary recordkeeping; contact us below to request deletion. This website has no added analytics tools, advertising, tracking cookies or contact form. The hosting provider may process access logs, including IP addresses and request information, to deliver and secure the pages.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Ledger records and photos remain on your device until you delete them or delete the app. Delete any copies in exported backups or other storage separately. Use device security and keep backups safe. You can change camera and photo permissions, adjust available advertising consent choices, and contact us with privacy or deletion requests. Google, Apple, hosting and email providers also apply their own policies and retention rules to information they process.

International processing and changes

Advertising, payment, hosting and email services may process technical or inquiry information outside your country. If app data practices change, this policy and its update date will be revised.
